How to Request Deletion

To delete your account and associated data for any of our apps (Notes, Financer, Productiv), send us a request using the link below. We will verify ownership and process your request within a reasonable period.

Email deletion request
If the link doesn't open your email client, email us at support@syntaxstudio.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

What Happens Next

  • We confirm ownership via your account email.
  • We permanently delete your data from our systems.
  • We'll notify you when deletion is complete.
  • If you have multiple apps, mention each one in your request.
