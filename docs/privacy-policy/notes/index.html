Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2025

App Name: Notes

Developer: Syntax Studio

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Notes ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

When you sign in with your email, Google, or other authentication methods, we collect your name, email address, and user ID from Firebase Authentication.

b. Notes and Content

We store your notes, titles, and any text content you create in the app in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore. These are private to your account unless you explicitly share them.

c. Device Information

We may collect non-identifiable data such as device type, operating system, and app version to improve performance and fix bugs.

d. Usage Data

We may collect anonymous analytics about app usage (such as how often you open the app) through Firebase Analytics or similar tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain your account and notes.
  • Improve app functionality and fix technical issues.
  • Communicate important updates or support messages.
  • Ensure your data is securely stored and accessible only to you.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore and protected by encryption. We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your data.

However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data. Your data is only shared with Firebase services necessary for the app to function (such as authentication and database management).

We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal processes.

5. Your Data Rights

  • Access and download your notes.
  • Delete your account and associated data permanently.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at: request@syntaxstudio.com or submit a request via our Request Account Deletion page.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all your personal data and notes are permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable period.

7. Third-Party Services

Our app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, including Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics). Please refer to the Firebase Privacy Policy for more information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe your child has provided us personal information, please contact us for removal.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted in the app or on our website with a new "Effective Date."

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@syntaxstudio.com
